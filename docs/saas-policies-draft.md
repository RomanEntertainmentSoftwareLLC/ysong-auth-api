# YSong SaaS policies — unpublished review draft

**ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED.** Version: `attorney-review-required`. These six policy records remain inactive and unapproved. This document is a review worksheet, not an approved public agreement. Publish separate immutable URLs and reviewed versions before changing the policy database configuration. Existing public pages do not automatically count as approved SaaS policies.

## Terms (`terms`)

Users must keep account credentials secure, supply accurate account information and use YSong lawfully. YSong may restrict access for documented abuse or infringement, with recorded review and restoration decisions. Counsel must finalize contracting entity/contact, age eligibility, territories, dispute procedure, warranty and liability language, termination and appeals. Do not promise uninterrupted service or ownership verification.

## Privacy (`privacy`)

The service processes account identifiers, saved projects and uploads, generation instructions and lyrics, provider/request metadata, subscriptions and policy acceptance records. Relevant existing processors include Cloudflare/R2, Neon, Vercel, Resend, Stripe and the configured generation providers. Credentials and payment instrument details must not be exposed in the browser. Counsel and the owner must confirm processor agreements, actual retention periods, lawful bases, international transfers, user rights, contact and applicable regional disclosures; no retention period is invented here.

## Upload and rights (`upload-rights`)

Uploaders attest that they have permission for the uses they request and identify a source or provenance reference. An attestation, hash, fingerprint or review decision does not prove legal ownership. Public use requires the existing ContentRightsGate and current moderation review. New or changed artwork enters manual review. Cleavage, swimwear, lingerie, shirtless people and non-explicit partial nudity are allowed; visible nipples/areola, genitalia, exposed pubic hair and explicit sexual content are blocked. Counsel must finalize license scope, appeals, prohibited material and rights-holder contact procedures.

## Billing and subscription (`billing`)

Intended monthly prices are Free $0, Basic $9.99, Pro $19.99 and Premium $29.99 USD. The public catalog shows configured allowances; blank quotas are unavailable, not unlimited. Checkout and account management use Stripe. Server-confirmed subscription events govern access. A browser redirect does not grant access. Cancellation and plan-switch behavior must match the configured customer portal. Counsel and the owner must finalize taxes, renewal notices, effective dates, refund policy, cancellation rights and support contact. No refund entitlement or timing is invented here.

## Generated output (`generated-output`)

Generated material may be similar to other material, may fail technical or quality checks and is not a guarantee of exclusivity, copyright protection or permission to use third-party content. Users should review outputs before publication. Persisted successful/usable versions consume their configured generation units; uncertain provider outcomes remain reserved for evidence-based review, with no blind paid retry or automatic refund. Counsel must verify provider-specific licenses and permitted commercial uses before publication.

## Bridge software license (`bridge-license`)

The Bridge provides local audio routing and plugin services. Users remain responsible for separately licensed plugins and authorized content. Counsel must finalize distribution/license terms, third-party notices, installation permissions, update and support commitments, liability and uninstall instructions. This pass does not ship or repackage the Bridge.

## Takedown operations before launch

The authenticated case form records a contact reference and claim for review; it does not certify statutory notice completeness. Decisions, counter-notices, prior removal counts and audit history are retained. Notification status starts pending; a human must deliver and record appropriate notices. Counsel must approve the designated-agent contact and registration, compliant notice/counter-notice requirements, delivery procedure, legal deadlines, preservation, litigation holds and repeat-infringer escalation. The product does not automatically restore after a counter-notice or automatically ban on a count.

Authoritative review references: [U.S. Copyright Office section 512 guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/512/index.html) and [designated-agent requirements](https://www.copyright.gov/title37/201/37cfr201-38.html). These requirements need an operational owner; the database case record alone is insufficient for launch.
